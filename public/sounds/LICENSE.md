Only concrete footsteps and mining/rock impacts are used; no synthesized audio.
Converted from the original Ogg Vorbis recordings using ffmpeg/libmp3lame,
96 kbps, mono, 44.1 kHz, metadata removed. MP3 was chosen for Chrome, Safari
(including versions without Ogg Vorbis), and Firefox compatibility:
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Formats/Audio_codecs#mp3_mpeg-1_audio_layer_iii
