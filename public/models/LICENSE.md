# Piece models — license

The twelve ARCANA piece models in this folder (`<type>-w.glb`, `<type>-b.glb`,
where type is `p`, `n`, `b`, `r`, `q`, or `k`) are © 2026 Wilmer Henao, authored
by the repository owner and processed by `scripts/process-models.mjs`.
The six `<type>.glb` files are bind-pose compatibility models without gameplay clips.
These are ARCANA V6 (queen, rook), V7 (bishop, pawn, king) and V8 (knight) models
with the owner's V4 static pedestals, simplified per rigid part and per dominant joint
of blended layers while every kept vertex retains its four skin weights. Gameplay
animation is resampled and meshopt-compressed alongside geometry. `arcana-materials.glb` contains the owner's shared PBR
materials/textures, resized and converted to WebP under the same attribution.
