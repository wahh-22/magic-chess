# H8 — ARCANA living chess
Reorients the whole game to the user's ARCANA low-poly collection: ivory vs obsidian articulated pieces with integrated weapons, authored natural attacks, a matching board, hall and light.
Branch `feat/h8-arcana` from main (652295f). The H6/H7 statue work stays on `feat/h6-medieval-army`; only ideas worth keeping are ported (slide-only motion, small-room framing).

## Specs
- S1 (L1): "hice los modelos aparte, este es el resultado, podemos incluirlo y orientar el juego a esto?" — the 12 ARCANA GLBs (`modelos/<pieza>_<marfil|obsidiana>.glb`: rey, reina, alfil, caballo, torre, peon) become the game's pieces: ivory = White, obsidian = Black, each side keeps its own materials (no tint).
- S2 (L2): "Todo: piezas, tablero, sala y luz" — board, hall and lighting restyled to match ARCANA (low poly, ivory/slate board with gold trim, cyan/gold magic accents), as in `vistas/tablero_completo.png` and `ARCANA_personajes.png`.
- S3 (L0 carried from H7, user: "como podemos lograr que sea aun mas realistas los movimientos. que el arma se vea mejor integrada, que los ataques se vean natural y no esforzados. es posible lograr eso, a nivel de juego profesional?") — attacks are authored per piece on the named bones (anticipation, strike, follow-through, recovery), the weapon is part of the model, impact lands on `plan.impact`, with hitstop and a weapon trail; no mesh deformation (rigid one-bone skinning).
- S4 (carried from H7 L2: "que no brinquen las fichas, sino que se deslicen por el tablero") — pieces slide, never hop; the knight slides its L.
- S5 — captured pieces break apart into their rigid armor components.
- S6 (L4): "vamos a actualzar a estos modelos que son mucho mas realistas: /Users/wilmerhenao/Downloads/ARCANA_Realista_3D.zip" — ARCANA Realismo V3 (plate-armor knights, PBR textures shared by all pieces, same rigid one-bone rigs) replaces the low-poly set, optimized for the web.
- S8 (L6): "Sala realista (recomendado)" — with realistic plate-armour pieces the hall and board become realistic too: PBR stone hall (the CC0 Poly Haven/ambientCG textures already in public/environment), realistic board (marble/stone squares, carved wooden or stone frame), braziers/torches with warm light plus restrained arcane accents, small enclosed room where pieces look big; lighting tuned so ivory steel reads silver and obsidian dark.
- S9 (L7): "estos son unos modelos con mejor realismo y movimiento, revisalos e implementemoslo" — ARCANA V4 (zips Alfil, Caballo, Peon, Reina, Rey, Torre, Complementos; Tablero not used: it is a 254 MB 32-piece scene) replaces V3: 71–101 bones with fingers, twist bones, weapon sockets/tips, cape chains, ~11 authored 60 Hz clips per piece with events (anticipation, weapon_trail_start, attack_active window, attack_end, recovery, footsteps, hit reactions), LOD1/LOD2, separate pedestals.
- S10 (L8): "Caminando (recomendado)" — pieces walk across the board with feet synchronized to the travel (knight at a walk or trot), turning to face the travel direction and back to face the opponent; nobody hops or skates.
- S11 (L8): "Reacciona y luego se desarma (recomendado)" — the captured piece plays its hit reaction at the weapon contact, then breaks apart into armour parts a few tenths of a second later.
- S12 (L8): "En guardia, respirando (recomendado)" — idle pieces loop a subtle guard/rest clip (desynchronized phases).
- S18 (L13): "si, dale con esos pasos. pero que se vea mas realista el ambiente, como en chess infinity. es que aqui se ve muy artificial. como podemos lograr ese realisto?" + choices "Sí, CC0 y CC BY (recomendado)" and "Hasta ~25 MB (recomendado)" — replace procedural ruins/vegetation with photoscanned assets (Poly Haven CC0 nature; CC BY scanned classical ruins with author credits), real alpha-cut foliage with wind, blended terrain with scattered rocks, contact shadows, god rays and height fog, more sky in the play view; environment download ≤ ~25 MB, lazy-loaded behind the menu with LODs and KTX2/meshopt. The other agreed steps follow: a sober medieval HUD (player bars, clock, moves) and more selectable environments.
- S17 (L12): "quiero que nuestro juego se vea como ese [Chess Infinity, Steam app 2828420]" + "A, y que no sea en un tablero pequeño, el nuestro esta orientedo al tamaño real, como un campo de batalla real" + "Ruinas antiguas al atardecer (recomendado)" — keep the life-size animated ARCANA armies and give the game Chess Infinity's presentation: the board is a large stone-paved battlefield plaza among ancient ruins at sunset (columns, hills, forest, warm low sun), photographic environment (HDRI sky/lighting + real geometry around the plaza), cinematic grading (filmic tone mapping, warm/teal grade, vignette), depth of field on close and cinematic shots, bloom, soft shadows and contact AO, ember/spark VFX on captures, atmospheric haze/dust motes; refined medieval HUD later.
- S16 (L11): "vamos a usar estos modelos mejorados y mas realistas" — ARCANA V6 (reina, torre: both sides per zip), V7 (alfil, peon, rey: one zip per side) and V8 (caballo per side, plus sources) replace V4: more detailed armour, closed-finger grips, events embedded in each GLB's animation extras, new attack sets (rook now strikes with a sword: Tajo_espada/Estocada_espada; bishop with a lance: Estocada_lanza/Corte_lanza; knight: Estocada_montada/Barrido_lanza; king/queen/pawn retimed); no LODs (130–220k triangles, knight 636k). Gliding (S15) stays.
- S15 (L10): "mejor vamos hacer que se deslicen, como estaba antes. no que caminen" — pieces glide in guard on their pedestal with the classic eased timing (knights slide their L); a capture glides in, briefly turns to face the victim, strikes with its authored attack, the victim reacts and breaks, then the attacker glides on and faces the opponent. Walking stays available as `locomotion: "walk"` (S10/S13 superseded by default).
- S13 (L9): "Bajan del pedestal y caminan por el tablero (recomendado)" — the pedestal stays on its square and fades out with a glow; the figure steps down, walks on the board, and a pedestal fades in under its feet on the destination square as it steps up.
- S14 (L9): "Más ágil, unos 3,5–4 s (recomendado)" — a one-square capture takes about 3.5–4 s end to end (brisker walk, shorter turns, final turn only when needed and overlapped with the arrival).
- S7 (L4): "los ataques que tambien sean mas realista, porfavor. y que los ataques se hagan con las armas siempre, amenos que no tenga armas. y que el movimiento del arma sea natural. como si fuera de verdad, y no tan robotizado" — every armed piece attacks with its weapon (king sword, queen scepter, bishop staff, knight lance, pawn spear; the unarmed rook punches); motion is natural, not robotic.

## Contract T10 ⇄ T11 (processed manifest, per piece type and color)
`rig.clips`: `idle` (Guardia, else Reposo / Reposo_montado; loop), `walk` (Caminar_en_sitio; knight Paso_en_sitio, plus `trot` Trote_en_sitio), `hit` (Impacto / Impacto_montado), `attacks` [{ clip, durationMs, hitMs (start of attack_active or the knight's attack event), activeEndMs, trailStartMs, trailEndMs }] (king Corte_espada + Estocada_espada; queen Golpe_baculo + Canalizar_hechizo; bishop Golpe_baculo + Canalizar_hechizo; knight Estocada_montada; rook Puno_pesado + Martillo_doble; pawn Estocada_lanza + Barrido_lanza), `walkCycle` { durationMs, strideBoardUnits } derived from the matching *_avance clip's root_motion_distance after board scaling, `weaponTip` bone name (from integration JSON tip_bone / sockets) for trails. Clip names in the GLB = the role keys above (idle, walk, trot, hit, attack0…). All times in ms at playback rate 1.
